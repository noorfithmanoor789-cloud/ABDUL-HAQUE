About

Mr. Abdul Haque Khoso is a distinguished nursing educator and researcher whose journey began in clinical practice at PMCH Nawabshah, where he developed a deep commitment to patient care and infection control. Transitioning into academia, he has since become a leading figure in nursing education, currently serving as Assistant Professor & Acting Principal at Sindhu Institute of Nursing, Khairpur Mirs, and as an active researcher at Peoples University of Medical & Health Sciences for Women (PUMHS), Shaheed Benazirabad. His work bridges clinical insight with scholarly rigour, shaping the next generation of nurses across Sindh.
